README.md

Complex requirements, software that stays understandable.

I am a Computer Science Engineer with 15+ years across web, backend, mobile and enterprise software: hospital emergency management, ERP/CRM implementations, multi-repository product platforms and, now, AI products. I am most comfortable where requirements are complex, integrations matter and the software has to stay understandable long after the first release.

I develop with AI coding agents every day, under my own open-source harness, ai-harness-framework. The agent writes code; I own the design, the review and the result.

Underneath the workflow: keep the architecture proportional to the problem, automate repetition rather than judgment, and understand the business process before the first line of code.

HARNESS.md

How I work with AI coding agents

The same loop runs for every feature. The agent can't skip a step, and neither can I.

  1. 01spec

    Spec first

    Scope, architecture and standards are written down before any code.

  2. 02feature

    One at a time

    Work is split into a feature list, with a contract for each step.

  3. 03gates

    Gates decide

    Lint, type check, tests, build and end-to-end must pass. A skipped check doesn't count as verified.

  4. 04log

    Decisions recorded

    Each non-obvious choice goes into a decisions log, so the reasoning outlives the session.

  5. 05review

    A human reviews

    Agent output is a draft until I have read it and accepted it.

The projects also test the framework. Building ai-invoice-intake turned up two bugs in the harness (D-021, D-022) and nine improvement issues, all fixed upstream.
